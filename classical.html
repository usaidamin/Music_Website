Great Composers

Johann Sebastian Bach

1685-1750 • Baroque

German composer and musician of the Baroque period, known for instrumental compositions such as the Brandenburg Concertos and the Goldberg Variations.

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

1756-1791 • Classical

Prolific and influential composer of the Classical era, composing over 600 works including symphonies, concertos, operas, and chamber music.

Ludwig van Beethoven

1770-1827 • Classical/Romantic

German composer whose works span the transition from the classical period to the romantic era in classical music.

Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky

1840-1893 • Romantic

Russian composer of the Romantic period, known for his ballets Swan Lake, The Nutcracker, and the 1812 Overture.

Historical Periods

1600-1750

Baroque

Characterized by ornate detail, contrast, and movement. Famous composers include Bach, Vivaldi, and Handel.

1750-1820

Classical

Emphasized clarity, balance, and form. Mozart, Haydn, and early Beethoven were key figures.

1820-1900

Romantic

Focused on emotion, individualism, and nature. Featured composers like Chopin, Tchaikovsky, and Brahms.

1900-Present

Modern/Contemporary

Experimentation with new forms, styles, and sounds. Includes Stravinsky, Shostakovich, and Glass.
