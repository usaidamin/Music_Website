Frequently Asked Questions

Artists can upload their music by creating an artist account on our platform. Once verified, you'll have access to our upload dashboard where you can submit your tracks, add metadata, and choose distribution options.

Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM and on Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We respond to emails within 24 hours during business days.

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used without authorization, please send us a detailed notice including the infringing material's location, your contact information, and a statement of good faith belief to our legal department at copyright@musichub.com.
